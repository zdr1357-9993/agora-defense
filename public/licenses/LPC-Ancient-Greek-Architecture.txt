The game background src/assets/greek-06.webp is an adaptation. Selected altar,
amphora, and Greek-key trim tiles were recolored, resized, softened, and composited
over the existing Agora background. The original asset sheet is included in this
folder. The rest of the background is from the project's existing artwork.
